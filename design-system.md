# Velmora design system

Direction: a Scottsdale editorial spa with the calm and considered detail of a small luxury hotel. Oversized Cormorant Garamond display, Manrope for practical information, warm bone, sand, espresso, restrained champagne. Hairline structure, open whitespace, natural skin imagery. Hero option selected: “A little refinement. Entirely you.”

The UI/UX skill recommendation was reviewed (research/design-recommendations.txt). Its default pink/lavender and Inter pairing conflict with the explicit specification, so the brief's warm neutrals and Cormorant direction take priority. Gold is decorative; dark gold-text token is used for readable text. Body copy is espresso or muted espresso on light surfaces; bone on espresso. Use tokens for all type and color.

Adaptations from every research source are recorded in research/ELEMENTS.md. Navigation is focused; prices explicit; quiz offers a low-pressure alternative. Native disclosure, dialog and keyboard-operable controls minimize interaction complexity. Motion respects reduced-motion, and informative copy never depends on the canvas.

Stock imagery uses the same warm grade at 480/960/1600 widths in AVIF and WebP. Models are explicitly illustrative. The original glass form uses a local HDRI and MeshTransmissionMaterial. No competitor assets ship.
