# Research shortlist

Selected by observed design and usability, not private conversion analytics. Six national design references (including facial specialists) and four Scottsdale rivals. Verified by web search and live Playwright CLI captures on September 24, 2026.

1. [SkinSpirit](https://www.skinspirit.com/) · National. Selected for treatment taxonomy. Evidence: [notes](sites/01-skinspirit/notes.md).
2. [Ever/Body](https://everbody.com/) · National. Selected for concern-first discovery. Evidence: [notes](sites/02-everbody/notes.md).
3. [Alchemy 43](https://alchemy43.com/) · National. Selected for personalized treatment framing. Evidence: [notes](sites/03-alchemy43/notes.md).
4. [JECT](https://ject.us/) · National. Selected for treatment detail hierarchy. Evidence: [notes](sites/04-ject/notes.md).
5. [Heyday](https://www.heydayskincare.com/) · National. Selected for visible membership math. Evidence: [notes](sites/05-heyday/notes.md).
6. [The Things We Do](https://thethingswedo.co/) · National. Selected for low-pressure quiz entry. Evidence: [notes](sites/06-thethingswedo/notes.md).
7. [Curated Medical](https://curatedmed.com/) · Scottsdale. Selected for hospitality-like atmosphere. Evidence: [notes](sites/07-curated-medical/notes.md).
8. [Inside Out Aesthetics](https://insideoutaesthetics.com/) · Scottsdale. Selected for accessible price navigation. Evidence: [notes](sites/08-inside-out/notes.md).
9. [The Comstock](https://thecomstock.com/) · Scottsdale. Selected for numbered treatment categories. Evidence: [notes](sites/09-the-comstock/notes.md).
10. [Institute of Aesthetics](https://www.instituteofaesthetics.com/) · Scottsdale. Selected for education as part of care.. Evidence: [notes](sites/10-institute-of-aesthetics/notes.md).

## Replacements and limitations
JECT corrected from ject.com to ject.us, and The Things We Do from .com to .co. Haus of Confidence was replaced because its hero embed displayed a connection-verification failure. Face Lounge was replaced after an obstructive form overlay impaired mobile review. Their captures remain under rejected/. Single dismissible promotions on otherwise useful national references were documented, dismissed and not adopted. Heyday is a facial specialist rather than a full medical spa, retained from the explicit seed list for its exceptional membership pattern.
