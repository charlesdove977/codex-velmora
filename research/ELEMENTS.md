## Positioning
All ten offer treatment discovery and an appointment path; most add provider credibility and repeat-care messaging. Several already combine clinical authority with warm interiors, so that alone is not a defensible gap. None of the captured home and inner-page samples combined an original educational 3D sequence, comparable five-part clinical specs, a clearly priced consultation flow and a searchable aftercare hub in one restrained experience. Velmora brings those pieces together under “Medical precision, hotel calm.” This is an observation about sampled pages, not a claim about every route on each competitor site.
