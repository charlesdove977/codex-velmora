A Balanced Transformation

What is Facial Balancing?

Facial balancing involves using dermal fillers and/or Botox to accentuate your facial contours and create a more symmetrical, proportionate look. Instead of treating just one area—like lips or cheeks—we take a holistic approach, ensuring all facial features are in harmony. This treatment can be used for contouring, volume restoration, and profile balancing, addressing concerns such as a receding chin, hollowing in the cheeks, asymmetry, or loss of definition over time.

A Whole-Face Approach

What is the Facial Balancing procedure like?

Our licensed medical providers begin with a personalized consultation to assess your facial structure and aesthetic goals. Treatment is then customized using a combination of:

  • Botox: Softens fine lines, rebalances facial expressions, slims the jawline, and enhances symmetry.
  • Fillers: Add volume, define contours, and restore youthful fullness in areas like the cheeks, chin, or lips.

Once a treatment plan has been created, the injectable procedure typically takes 30-60 minutes. Our experienced providers administer dermal fillers or Botox in precise areas to accentuate your features naturally and harmoniously. Most people find the treatment very tolerable, but a topical numbing cream can be applied to make the injection process more comfortable.

The Power of Proportion

What conditions does Facial Balancing address?

By refining multiple areas in one session, facial balancing provides a harmonious result that looks natural to you. Depending on your needs, your treatment may address any of the following:

  • Facial asymmetry – Creates a more harmonious, proportionate appearance.
  • Receding or weak chin – Enhances jawline projection and profile balance.
  • Flat, hollow, or undefined cheeks – Restores volume and enhances contour.
  • Deep nasolabial folds or marionette lines – Smooths and softens the mid-to-lower face.
  • Aging-related volume loss – Restores youthful fullness and definition.

Smooth, Contoured, and Balanced

What are the benefits of facial balancing?

Facial balancing creates a refined yet natural enhancement, focusing on harmony and proportion rather than exaggerated changes. Sometimes, treating a single feature can make it obvious that someone has "had work done," whereas overall facial balancing tends to yield a more natural and subtle result.

This minimally invasive approach provides balanced rejuvenation with no incisions, scars, or extended downtime. Patients see improvements right away, with results lasting 6-12 months. Our licensed medical providers are facial balancing experts, customizing the treatment to your unique features for precise, artistic placement. Our balanced approach ensures subtle, refined enhancements.

Ready for a Subtle Refresh?

Am I a candidate for facial balancing?

Facial balancing is ideal for anyone looking to enhance facial symmetry, definition, or overall harmony without surgery. You may be a great candidate if you:

  • Want a more structured jawline or defined profile
  • Have mild to moderate asymmetry
  • Are looking for natural-looking, subtle enhancements
  • Want to restore lost volume and youthful contouring

Those with certain allergies or medical conditions should consult our licensed medical providers before treatment.

Minimally Invasive. No Downtime.

Facial Balancing Recovery & Results

Since facial balancing is a minimally-invasive treatment, there is little to no downtime. Some mild swelling or bruising may occur but typically resolves within a few days.

To ensure optimal results:

  • Avoid intense exercise for 24 hours.
  • Refrain from touching or massaging treated areas.
  • Stay hydrated and follow post-care instructions from your provider.

Results typically last for 6-12 months, depending on the types of injectables used. Touch-up treatments can be scheduled periodically to extend your results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does facial balancing look natural?

Yes, the goal of facial balancing is to create subtle, harmonious enhancements that highlight your natural features.

How many syringes of filler do I need for facial balancing?

The amount of filler varies based on your facial structure and goals, but most treatments range from 1-4 syringes strategically placed for the most natural effect.

How long does facial balancing take?

The procedure itself takes 30-60 minutes, with no significant downtime.

Can I get Botox and fillers on the same day?

Yes! Botox and fillers are often combined for the best results, ensuring a balanced, refreshed look.

Is facial balancing painful?

Most clients experience minimal discomfort, thanks to numbing agents in the fillers and the expert precision of our licensed medical providers.

Are facial balancing treatments eligible for Allē points?

Yes, this service counts towards your Allē points.
