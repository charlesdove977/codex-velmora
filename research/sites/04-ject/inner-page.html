Deep Peel

Intense Pulsed Light (IPL)

Resurfacing Laser (Moxi)

Skin Rejuvenation

PRP & PRFM Facial Rejuvenation

Using collagen-stimulating growth factors from your Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) or Platelet Rich Fibrin Matrix (PRFM), areas such as the undereyes are treated to improve skin texture and dark circles.

Hair Rejuvenation

PRP & PRFM Hair Treatments

Using concentrated growth factors from your own Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) or Platelet Rich Fibrin Matrix (PRFM), targeted areas are injected to stimulate hair growth and improve thinning hair.

Sofwave™
