A New Approach to Skincare

Safety

Safety is our top priority. Our injectors are all licensed medical professionals specializing in medical aesthetics, who have completed JECT Academy, our rigorous training program developed in partnership with our Medical Directors.

Efficacy

We are firm believers that medical-grade services are the way to go in order to see results now and in the future. Our services are curated to deliver results –– active ingredients mean visible results.

Experience

We provide a carefully curated experience. Appointments follow a signature format, yet each is personalized based on clients' needs. Our team will work with you to create an individualized treatment plan that’s perfect for you.
