Picosure Pro
$550 to $2,925
+

*Available in DTLA, Venice, Chino Hills, and O'ahu.

PicoSure® Pro with the Focus™ Lens Array is a powerful, non-invasive laser treatment that targets pigmentation, acne scars, and uneven texture—while also visibly tightening and revitalizing the skin. This advanced 755nm picosecond laser delivers ultra-short bursts of energy to gently break up discoloration and stimulate collagen and elastin production beneath the surface.

The addition of the Focus™ Lens allows the energy to penetrate deeper into the skin, triggering a natural healing response that helps smooth fine lines, refine pores, and improve overall firmness without damaging surrounding tissue.

Now available for the body as well, this treatment can address concerns beyond the face—including surgical scars, hyperpigmentation, body acne marks, sun damage, and more—making it a versatile option for full-body skin rejuvenation.

Safe for all skin tones and requiring little to no downtime, this treatment is perfect for anyone seeking brighter, clearer, more youthful-looking skin—without harsh peels or needles.

BioGlow
$2,780
+

If your skin is starting to feel thinner, less firm, or just not as vibrant as it used to be, BioGlow is designed to restore what’s been lost—naturally and gradually.

BioGlow combines two powerful biostimulators: PRFM (Platelet-Rich Fibrin Matrix) and Sculptra. Together, they work beneath the surface to rebuild collagen, improve skin quality, and restore structure over time.

PRFM uses your body’s own growth factors to rejuvenate and thicken the skin—helping improve texture, elasticity, and overall skin health. Sculptra complements this by stimulating long-term collagen production, restoring firmness and subtle volume while supporting the skin’s underlying framework.

This combination treatment is ideal for those looking for a more comprehensive, regenerative approach—targeting both skin quality and structural support in one session.

Expect a gradual, natural-looking improvement in firmness, smoothness, and overall radiance. Results build over time and continue to improve for months after treatment.

BioGlow includes PRFM and 2 vials of Sculptra.

PRFM
$1,080 to $3,315
+

PRFM stands for Platelet-Rich Fibrin Matrix and is used to thicken thinning skin and create a naturally youthful, refreshed appearance. Blood is drawn from your arm, spun through a centrifuge, and thickened to create a more powerful bio-regenerative treatment. The gel growth factor is injected back into your skin where it needs it most. Results last between 1-2 years.

PRFM with Glass Skin Stamping
$1,300+
+

For anyone wanting brighter, firmer, smoother, pore-minimizing skin with a true glass-skin finish—this Korea-favorite treatment delivers exactly that. PRFM (Platelet-Rich Fibrin Matrix) will be stamped into your skin to deliver your body’s own growth factors directly into the skin. The stamping technique creates microchannels that enhance absorption and stimulate collagen production, improving texture, tone, and elasticity from within.

Beloved for its ability to refine pores, smooth texture, and create a radiant, hydrated, luminous complexion, this treatment continues to improve as new collagen develops.

Consider purchasing a series for the best results.
