At Institute of Aesthetics in Scottsdale, Arizona, we specialize in natural-looking injectables, advanced laser skin treatments, regenerative aesthetics, and medical wellness services designed to help you look refreshed, feel confident, and age beautifully from the inside out.

Our Philosophy

At Institute of Aesthetics, our philosophy is rooted in the balance of advanced medical expertise, artistic precision, and highly individualized care. As a luxury boutique med spa and medical wellness center in Scottsdale, AZ, we take a thoughtful, medically guided approach to facial rejuvenation, skin health, laser treatments, regenerative aesthetics, and long-term aging support.

Our goal is never to change the way you look. Instead, we design personalized treatment plans that support facial harmony, natural-looking results, skin quality, and confidence through every stage of aging.

We believe aesthetic medicine should enhance your natural beauty — never erase what makes you uniquely you.

Why Patients Choose Us

  • Our Scottsdale med spa is led by a team of advanced aesthetic medical providers trained in injectables, facial rejuvenation, laser treatments, skin health, regenerative aesthetics, and medical wellness. Under the clinical leadership of Jennifer Prince, FNP-C, CANS, our team takes an anatomy-based, medically guided approach to safety, natural-looking results, and long-term aesthetic planning.

  • We specialize in subtle facial rejuvenation, Botox and Dysport, dermal fillers, Sculptra, laser skin treatments, and regenerative aesthetics designed to refresh your appearance without looking overdone.

  • Every patient receives a customized plan based on facial anatomy, skin health, lifestyle, aging patterns, and long-term goals. Our approach is never one-size-fits-all.

  • From BBL, Moxi, microneedling, facials, and regenerative therapies to medical weight loss and hormone optimization, our approach supports both how you look and how you feel.

Our Providers

Our team of advanced aesthetic professionals works under the direct clinical leadership of founder Jennifer Prince, FNP-C, CANS. With decades of combined experience in skin health and injectable aesthetics, every provider brings specialized training, a commitment to safety, and a shared philosophy centered on precision and natural results.

The Institute of Aesthetics team includes Certified Aesthetic Nurse Specialists, Functional Medicine Practitioners, Licensed Medical Aestheticians, and Certified Laser Technologists — each an expert in their discipline, all aligned in their approach.

Our Founder

Jennifer Prince, FNP-C, CANS, is a nationally recognized aesthetic nurse practitioner with over 18 years of experience in plastic surgery, aesthetic medicine, and skin health. Known for her refined, medically guided approach, Jennifer specializes in creating natural, balanced results that enhance each patient’s unique features rather than change them.

As the founder of Institute of Aesthetics, she blends advanced clinical expertise with an artistic eye to develop individualized treatment plans focused on long-term skin health, facial harmony, and graceful aging. Her philosophy centers on thoughtful, conservative care — helping patients look refreshed, confident, and unmistakably themselves.

In addition to leading her Scottsdale practice, Jennifer contributes to the advancement of the aesthetics industry through advanced professional education, mentorship, and online training designed for experienced providers seeking elevated clinical insight.
