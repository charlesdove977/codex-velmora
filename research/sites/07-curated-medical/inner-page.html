FAQ’s
How often should I get injectable treatments?
We recommend neuromodulators (Botox, Dysport, Daxxify) every 3-4 months. Filler is typically around once a year but may be more or less depending on the area.
What kind of injectable treatments are available?
We offer neuromodulators (Botox, Dysport and Daxxify) which treat fine lines and wrinkles caused by muscle movement. Typical treatment areas include forehead lines, frown lines and crows feet. We also offer hyaluronic acid fillers (Juvederm, Restylane, Revanesse, and RHA) which are gel fillers that create contour, balance facial features, and revolumize. Additionally, we offer collagen-stimulating fillers like Sculptra and hyperdilute Radiesse. These are going to give you little all-over natural volume and also improve skin quality.
Which injectable treatment is right for me?
We provide in-depth consultations to create a plan that will provide you the best results based on your anatomy and your goals.
What are the side effects of injectable treatments?
Bruising, swelling and soreness can be common with filler. Occasionally you may get a small bruise after neuromodulator treatment as well. Typically these are not super noticeable but we always recommend giving yourself at least 2 weeks if you have a big event coming up.
Do all injectables cost the same?
We charge per area for filler, typically this is $700-850 per syringe. Neuromodulators typically range in price (depending on the areas treated) from $600-800. We do offer discounts through our membership program!
