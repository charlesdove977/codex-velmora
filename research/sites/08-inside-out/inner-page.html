Treatments

HydraFacials are one of the most popular treatments we offer at Inside Out Aesthetics.

This revolutionary skin treatment uses a patented device that will exfoliate, cleanse, extract, and hydrate the face. A vortex swirling action delivers hydration and removes dead skin, dirt, debris, and impurities, all while cleaning and soothing your skin.

Results

After your HydraFacial treatment, your skin will feel incredibly smooth. Your complexion will appear more hydrated, bright, and clear. The treatment reduces fine lines and wrinkles, increases firmness, evens skin tone, texture, and brown spots, as well as reducing enlarged pores. Ask to see the “stuff” that gets cleaned out of your skin. You’ll be amazed!

Before

What to Expect

Before undergoing your HydraFacial, you’ll need to attend a consultation with your provider at Inside Out Aesthetics. They will discuss your appearance goals, skin concerns, and medical history to determine whether HydraFacial is right for you.

While HydraFacials are suitable for most people regardless of skin tone and type, you may not be a good candidate if you are pregnant, or have active rashes, sunburns, rosacea, or open wounds.

Conditions Treated

Getting a HydraFacial in Scottsdale can treat a comprehensive range of skin concerns, including:

Signs of Aging

HydraFacial treatments can help reduce signs of aging by softening fine lines and wrinkles while boosting skin elasticity and firmness for a more youthful, refreshed appearance.

Uneven Texture

The deep exfoliation of HydraFacial can also help smooth rough or uneven areas for a more uniform texture.

Hyperpigmentation

HydraFacials are highly effective at targeting hyperpigmentation or uneven skin tone. Whether you’re concerned about brown spots or sun damage, the treatment can help minimize their appearance.

Acne

During your HydraFacial treatment, your skin will be deeply cleansed and infused with nourishing serums that can help reduce the appearance of acne and regulate the frequency and severity of blemishes.

Congested Pores

HydraFacials utilize powerful vacuum-like cleansing technology to extract debris from pores, minimizing their appearance and reducing congestion.

How it works

HydraFacial

The HydraFacial treatment is a three-step process, which includes:

Cleanse and peel

Your provider will first gently exfoliate your skin with a cleansing peel containing salicylic and glycolic acids to remove dead skin cells and uncover a new, healthy layer of skin.

Extract and hydrate

In a painless and comfortable process, your provider will use the HydraFacial device to suction debris from pores and nourish the skin with intensive moisturizers. The spiral tip design of the HydraFacial wand ensures these beneficial ingredients penetrate deeply into the skin for maximum effectiveness.

Fuse and protect

The final step of the HydraFacial treatment involves applying antioxidants and peptides to the skin to enhance youthful results.

 

Benefits

Hydrafacials can help you achieve a refreshed, radiant complexion and improve common skin concerns like wrinkles and fine lines, congested pores, and uneven tone. Many patients are opting for this treatment due to its unique benefits, which include:

Protects the Natural Skin Barrier

Unlike other facial treatments that can leave skin feeling stripped or irritated, HydraFacials work by simultaneously cleansing and hydrating the skin. By maintaining the skin’s moisture barrier, you can still enjoy the deep, exfoliating effects of a facial without worrying about aggravating your skin.

Highly Customizable

The skin-nourishing ingredients used in HydraFacial treatments can be personalized to treat your unique needs. From sensitive to dry to oily, the treatment is suitable for all skin types.

Quick and Easy Treatment

The entire HydraFacial treatment typically takes just 30 minutes, is painless, and requires no downtime, making it ideal for patients with a busy schedule. While some patients may experience mild redness after the treatment, it often subsides quickly.

Immediate Results

Unlike some other aesthetic treatments, the results of your HydraFacial will be visible right away. You won’t have to wait to enjoy your luminous, healthy glow and show off your rejuvenated look.

After

What to Expect

There is no downtime after a HydraFacial, meaning you can get back to regular activities right after your appointment, but with a fresh, radiant glow! However, you should still take certain precautions, like protecting the skin from excess sun exposure. 

You’ll get to enjoy the fantastic results of your treatment right away, and they will likely last for around five to seven days or more. For best long-term results, many providers recommend a monthly maintenance schedule. 

However, treatment frequency will be determined based on your individual skincare needs and goals. There are certain steps you can take to maintain the results of your HydraFacial for longer, including: 

  • Avoiding tanning beds 

  • Getting enough sleep 

  • Prioritizing a healthy, balanced diet 

  • Staying hydrated 

  • Taking vitamins and supplements as directed by your provider 

  • Keeping your stress levels low 

  • Avoiding drinking and smoking

FAQs

Can anyone of any skin type get a HydrFacial?

Yes, we can alter the serums to fit your skin needs!

Is there any downtime with HydraFacial?

Not at all, you'll be able to return to normal activity after your treatment.

Should I get a dermaplane beforehand?

We always recommend a dermaplane before any facial. Dermaplaning creates a nice exfoliation of the skin and gets rid of "peach fuzz". This helps to maximize the results of a facial by allowing the products to penetrate at a deeper level.
