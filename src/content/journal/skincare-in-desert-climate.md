Desert light is beautiful. Dry air and regular sun exposure can also make skin care feel less straightforward. When skin feels tight or irritated, adding more active products is not always the answer.

A comfortable routine begins with a few consistent basics. Your skin type, medications and any recent procedures can change what those basics look like.

## Keep cleansing gentle

A cleanser should remove the day without leaving your skin uncomfortable. Hot water, rough scrubs and frequent exfoliation can add irritation. If your routine stings, simplify it and speak with a qualified clinician rather than pushing through.

## Make room for moisture

The right moisturizer depends on your skin. A light texture may suit one person, while another needs a richer product. Introduce one change at a time so you can notice how your skin responds.

A [facial consultation](/treatments/hydrafacial) can help you discuss a routine, but an in-practice treatment does not replace everyday care.

## Treat sun protection as a habit

The American Academy of Dermatology recommends broad-spectrum, water-resistant SPF 30 or higher, used alongside shade and protective clothing. Reapply as directed, particularly when outdoors, swimming or sweating.

After a peel or laser treatment, follow the specific instructions from your provider. Healing skin may need a different plan. Our [aftercare guides](/support) explain the questions to ask.

Read the [AAD sun-protection guidance](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/sun-damage-skin-protection) for more detail. New, changing or bleeding spots should be assessed by a medical professional.
