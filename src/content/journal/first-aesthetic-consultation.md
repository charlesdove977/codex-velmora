A first aesthetic consultation should help you understand your options. You do not need to arrive knowing the name of a treatment or ready to commit to one.

Start with what you notice and what matters to you. You might want softer expression lines, a more even tone or simply a routine that feels manageable. These are useful starting points for a conversation.

## Bring the whole picture

Your clinician needs more than a photograph. Bring a list of medicines and supplements, details of previous procedures and your current skincare products. Mention allergies, healing problems and any history of changes in skin color after an injury.

If you have had filler, share the product, location and approximate date if you know them. A procedure from several years ago may still matter to a new plan.

## Ask about the practical details

A useful plan explains the likely benefit, important risks, alternatives and the option of doing nothing. Ask how the treatment fits your skin tone and health history. Ask who performs it, what their qualifications are and how the practice handles complications.

Recovery deserves equal attention. A short appointment does not always mean a short recovery. Discuss work, exercise, travel and events before choosing a date.

## Leave with a plan you understand

Request a written estimate and clear follow-up instructions. You can take time to consider the information. A consultation does not obligate you to have treatment.
